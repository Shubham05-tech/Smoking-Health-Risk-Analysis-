# 🫀 Smoking Health Risk Analysis Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Smoking Health Risk Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze the relationship between smoking habits and different health risk factors.

The dashboard provides insights into patient demographics, smoking status, age groups, BMI, cholesterol, hypertension, and smoking-related factors.

It helps users understand patterns and identify health-risk trends among different patient groups.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze patients based on their smoking status.
- Compare health indicators across different age groups.
- Analyze smoking habits such as cigarettes consumed per day and years of smoking.
- Identify patterns related to cholesterol and hypertension.
- Compare health-risk factors between male and female patients.
- Provide an interactive dashboard for quick health-risk analysis.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI** – Dashboard development and visualization
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** – Measures and calculations
- **Microsoft Excel / CSV** – Data source
- **Data Visualization** – Charts, KPIs, and interactive filters

---

## 📌 Dashboard Features

### 🏥 Health Category Navigation
The dashboard includes different health categories:

- ❤️ Heart
- 🧍 Human
- 🫘 Kidney
- 🫀 Liver
- 🫁 Lungs

### 🚬 Smoking Status Analysis

The dashboard analyzes patients based on:

- Never smoked
- Current smokers
- Former smokers

A donut chart shows the distribution of patients across smoking categories.

### 👥 Demographic Analysis

The dashboard provides analysis based on:

- Age groups
- Gender
- Smoking status

### 🚬 Smoking Behavior

The dashboard analyzes:

- Cigarettes consumed per day
- Years of smoking

across different age groups.

### 🩸 Cholesterol & Hypertension Risk

A stacked column chart is used to analyze the distribution of:

- High BP risk
- Low BP risk
- Normal BP

across different age groups.

---

## 📈 Key KPIs

The dashboard displays important metrics such as:

- Total Patient Count
- Average Age
- Average BMI
- Smoking Status Distribution
- Smoking Duration
- Cigarettes Per Day
